# ForceIA Visual Identity

## Recognition without logo
Signatures that should remain even if the “F” mark is removed:

1. **Brand signal rail** — 2px cyan vertical on live/AI surfaces
2. **Ops brief** — one narrative sentence of what the AI team did today
3. **Agent stage** — three live AI services as the primary product surface
4. **Mono ops codes** — QUAL · MTG · FUP · HO · PIPE in IBM Plex Mono
5. **Live pulse** — green dot + “Em operação” on active agents
6. **Process bars** — thin brand-colored load under each agent
7. **Interrupt lane** — “Precisa de você” with warning border when handoffs exist

## Narrative order (Dashboard)
1. How is the operation? (OpsBrief)
2. Who is working? (AgentStage)
3. What needs me? (Attention)
4. What changed? (Chart + KPIs)
5. What did AI do / what’s next? (Log + Agenda)

## Not CRM
- No pipeline kanban as home
- No contact-grid first
- Agents are processes, not user avatars
- Data supports the story; it is not the story
